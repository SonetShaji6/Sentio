# 1. PRODUCT DEFINITION

## 1.1 What is Sentio?

Sentio is an AI-powered interactive live presentation platform.

A presenter creates a presentation containing multiple slides.

Slides can contain:

* Information
* Polls
* Quizzes
* Ratings
* Word clouds
* Open text
* Q&A
* Images
* Other interactive elements

During a live presentation:

Presenter
↓
Starts session
↓
Audience joins using QR/code
↓
Audience waits
↓
Presenter starts
↓
Interactive slide becomes active
↓
Audience responds
↓
Presenter sees live participation
↓
Results are revealed
↓
Quiz scores are calculated
↓
Leaderboard appears
↓
AI analyzes audience interaction
↓
AI gives presenter recommendations
↓
Presenter decides what to do
↓
Next slide
↓
Session analytics
↓
AI insights
↓
Report

This is the core Sentio experience.

---

# 2. PRODUCT POSITIONING

Sentio should feel like a combination of:

* Interactive presentation software
* Mentimeter-style live interaction
* Kahoot-style competitive quizzes
* AI-powered presentation assistant

But Sentio must have its own identity.

The product should NOT feel like a clone.

The unique differentiator is:

> Sentio understands what the audience is doing during a presentation and helps the presenter make better decisions in real time.

# 3. PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

The core product loop is:

### Presenter creates

↓

### Audience interacts

↓

### Sentio collects real-time signals

↓

### Sentio analyzes the audience

↓

↓

↓

AI assists the presenter.

# 4. PRIMARY ACCEPTANCE SCENARIO

This scenario is the most important test of the entire application.

A new user:

1. Signs up.
2. Verifies email.
3. Logs in.
4. Reaches Dashboard.
5. Creates a presentation.
6. Adds a Welcome slide.
7. Adds 10 MCQ quiz slides.
8. Adds one Poll slide.
9. Configures quiz timers.
10. Configures scoring.
11. Configures leaderboard visibility.
12. Uses AI to generate answer options.
13. Reviews and edits AI suggestions.
14. Saves presentation.
15. Previews presentation.
16. Starts a live session.
17. Sees QR code and session code.
18. Participants scan/join.
19. Participants enter their names.
20. Presenter sees live participant count.
21. Presenter starts presentation.
22. Question 1 appears.
23. Participants answer.
24. Presenter sees live answer count.
25. Timer runs.
26. Responses lock.
27. Correct answer is revealed.
28. Answer distribution appears.
29. Top 5 leaderboard appears.
30. Scores update.
31. Next question starts.
32. Process continues.
33. Poll slide starts.
34. Poll responses update live.
35. AI analyzes audience behavior.
36. AI provides a meaningful recommendation.
37. Presenter decides whether to follow it.
38. Presentation continues.
39. Session ends.
40. Analytics are generated.
41. AI insights are generated.
42. Report becomes available.

If this workflow does not feel polished, Sentio is not complete.

# 18. TIME-BASED SCORING

Define the exact scoring algorithm.

Requirements:

* Correct answer required
* Faster correct answers receive higher scores
* Incorrect answers receive zero or configured penalty
* Timeout receives zero
* Score is deterministic
* Score is calculated server-side
* Score is testable

Document:

* Formula
* Inputs
* Boundaries
* Rounding
* Minimum/maximum score
* Tie handling

Do not implement an undocumented scoring algorithm.

# 23. AI LIVE ANALYSIS

Define exactly when AI runs.

Do NOT send every interaction to Groq individually.

Possible triggers:

* Response threshold reached
* Question completed
* Poll completed
* Significant engagement change
* Multiple consecutive difficult questions
* End of section

AI input should contain aggregated context.

Example:

Current question:

Question 4

Participants:

32

Answered:

30

Correct:

11

Incorrect:

19

B — 15 responses

Average response time:

16.2s

Previous question accuracy:

68%

Current accuracy:

37%

AI then produces structured output:

INSIGHT

RECOMMENDATION

EVIDENCE

CONFIDENCE

# 39. VISUAL QUALITY GATE

A UI change is NOT considered successful merely because:

* Tailwind classes changed
* Colors changed
* Animations were added
* Components were reorganized

The redesigned UI must improve:

* Information hierarchy
* Discoverability
* Clarity
* Speed
* Feedback
* Consistency
* Accessibility
* Responsiveness

Avoid:

* Excessive gradients
* Excessive glassmorphism
* Unnecessary 3D
* Distracting animations
* Tiny controls
* Overloaded dashboards
* Decorative UI that competes with presentation content

---

# 40. DO NOT OVER-ENGINEER

Do not introduce technologies merely because they are popular.

Do not introduce:

* New databases
* New queues
* New state management libraries
* New AI providers
* New realtime systems

unless the current architecture demonstrably requires them.

Reuse existing infrastructure.
